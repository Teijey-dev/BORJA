These are some of the photos I have taken, capturing the sky in its different colors and moods. From soft shades of blue to warm hues of orange, pink, and purple, each photo reflects a unique moment of beauty that I was fortunate enough to witness. I find something peaceful and captivating about looking at the sky, especially when its colors change throughout the day. Through these photographs, I hope to share a little of the beauty I see and the quiet moments that make ordinary days feel extraordinary.

This photo was taken during the transfer day of our beloved adviser, who was assigned to another school. On that day, we did our best to make her farewell special and memorable. Although we were sad to see her leave, we wanted to show how much we appreciated her guidance, kindness, and support throughout our time together. It was a day filled with laughter, happiness, and a few tears.

This photo of my family was taken during our island hopping to Naked Island, where the beautiful sea meets the horizon. It was a wonderful moment spent together, enjoying the fresh breeze, clear waters, and peaceful surroundings. Being with my family made the experience even more special because we were able to create memories that we will always cherish.

This photo was taken on the day we were announced as the overall champions of our school's intramurals. It was a moment filled with excitement, happiness, and pride because all our hard work and efforts had finally paid off. Throughout the competition, we showed teamwork, determination, and sportsmanship while supporting one another.

The first photo was taken during our Drum and Lyre Corps (DLC) competition. It captures one of the memorable moments of our journey as a group, where we showcased our talents, discipline, and teamwork. Preparing for the competition required a lot of practice, patience, and dedication from everyone. Being part of this group taught me the importance of cooperation, commitment, and working together toward one goal. The second photo was taken during our journalism competition in Burgos, Siargao. It was a wonderful experience where we had the opportunity to meet other student journalists, learn new things, and improve our skills in journalism. Being part of this group allowed me to explore my interest in storytelling through photography which is photojournalism. The third and last photo was taken on the day our Sineliksik group won second place in the competition. It was a proud and unforgettable moment for all of us because our hard work, creativity, and teamwork had finally paid off. The journey was not always easy, as we faced challenges while preparing our work, but we never gave up.
